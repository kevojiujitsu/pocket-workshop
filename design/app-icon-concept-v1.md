# App icon concept 1

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool and approved by the owner. Installed in Android launcher resources (all five densities, legacy round and adaptive foregrounds) and the iOS AppIcon asset catalog. `export-icons.py` reproduces exports without changing the artwork. Android debug build verified. Apple App Store icon is supplied by a future uploaded iOS build; that build cannot be produced on this Windows host.

Prompt:

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished square app icon concept for Pocket Workshop, a cozy but challenging sliding-block repair puzzle game. A charming small brass-gold repair toolbox is the clear central symbol, nested among a few chunky sliding wooden puzzle blocks, subtly suggesting a 6-by-6 workbench without rendering a whole busy board. Deep forest green background (#203e38), sage green and warm cream blocks, muted brass gold hero object. Tactile softly rounded crafted materials, refined illustrated 3D, soft warm light and subtle shadows, cozy workshop feeling. Strong simple silhouette and high contrast readable at tiny phone icon size. Centered composition with generous safe margins, edge-to-edge opaque square background; no rounded outer app-icon mask, no border, no typography, no words, no letters, no watermark. Deliver a single 1024x1024 icon, not a mockup or contact sheet.

Google Play: 512px icon uploaded and saved in the default en-US store-listing draft. Not submitted for review or published.
